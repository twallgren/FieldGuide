Most of them are trades. Some of them are not worth it.

Almost everything written about AI workflows adds to the pile — another technique, another tool, another thing you are now behind on. This does the opposite.

Every technique here is scored on four signed axes against a stated baseline, carries an explicit confidence level, records the sources that disagree, and is allowed to conclude that it did not earn its place.

Nothing here is a dated post. Each entry is maintained: when evidence arrives that changes an assessment, the score changes and the change is on the record — including where I was wrong.

The four axes

The one rule
+ is always better for you.
On every axis.

cost-delta: +2 means much cheaper. Not “much cost.”

attention-delta: −1 means it demands more of your attention.

cost-delta
Tokens per accepted outcome
How much of the budget did the outcome consume — retries included, not tokens per call?
Binds when cost-bound
quality-delta
Accuracy & durability
Is the artifact actually better — including the parts nobody said out loud, and six months from now?
Binds when quality-bound
speed-delta
Wall-clock to accepted
How long from start to accepted? Faster output you trust less is not faster.
Binds when latency-bound
attention-delta
Interventions & context switches
How much of you did it consume? The most common bad trade is a technique that looks cheaper only because it moved work onto you.

02

Three classes

Two of the three classes fall out of the delta vector on their own. Dial never does — a technique that hands you a knob looks unremarkable in its scores — so it is marked by hand, and it is the class worth hunting for.

NO-REGRET
6
Nothing gets worse. Adopt unconditionally — no regime needed, no argument required.
Cache-Stable Agent Thread Architecture · Composable Tests · Skills vs MCP Servers
TRADE
12
Spends one axis to buy another. Only evaluable against a regime — correct in one, indefensible in the next.
Multi-Agent Debate · Low-Cost Model Swarms · Fine-Tuning Open-Weight Models
DIAL
9
Hands you a per-task knob you did not have. Worth more than either endpoint, because regimes change inside a single day.
Model Routing · Hedged LLM Requests · Deterministic Agent Workflows
03

Two disciplines that make this falsifiable

Every delta is against a stated baseline.

A delta is meaningless without compared to what. Every evaluation names its baseline in a block you cannot scroll past, before any number appears.

The scale is ordinal. It is never summed.

Two +1s do not equal a +2. There is no overall rating here and there will not be one. If a summary number starts to feel necessary, that is a signal the regime was never specified.
